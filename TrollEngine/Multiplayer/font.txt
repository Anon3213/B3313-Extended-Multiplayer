; The font the game draws players' names and the chat in: this project's own, in place of
; B3313's dialog font, whose letters are all capitals and run into each other on purpose.
;
; Each glyph is drawn here as it appears on the screen, `#` for its pixels and `.` for none,
; in a box up to 5 pixels wide and 10 high. Rows count from the top: capitals and tall letters
; fill rows 0 to 7, small letters rows 2 to 7, and tails hang into rows 8 and 9. Rows left off
; the bottom are empty. scripts/make_font.py gives every glyph a dark outline one pixel wide
; and packs them into font.bin, which the ROM carries and the bridge measures text with; run
; it after changing anything here, and look at the result with its --preview.
;
; A glyph is as wide as its rows, which must all be the same length, and the next one starts
; one pixel after it, so their outlines share a column.
